IThe Signals
the fast read — one move each
Gaussian splats just became consumer-map infrastructure.
At WWDC on Monday, Apple quietly rebuilt Flyover in Apple Maps on top of 3D Gaussian Splatting — over 350 cities getting radiance-field 3D this fall. Apple wouldn't say the words out loud, but Radiance Fields and every splat researcher in the room confirmed it. This pulls splats out of the lab and into a default consumer surface, and the entire web3D stack we already build on just got a billion-device tailwind.
lead every gcc pitch this month with a real splat of a real landmark.
Vision Pro opens to third-party tracked props and controllers.
visionOS 27, in dev hands since Monday, introduces a formal Spatial Accessories framework — Apple now actively tracks third-party motion controllers, branded props, and LED-marked physical objects. Until now, only passive object recognition was supported. This turns Vision Pro from a pure hand-tracking device into a hardware ecosystem — and the bundle we sell can now include a custom-tracked object, not just software.
design a branded tracked prop spec sheet for the next experiential pitch.
NEOM's flagship Line just got shelved until 2030.
NEOM issued a temporary work stoppage on The Line until at least 2030 and is reportedly paying a $16B exit bill to contractors being released this month. Capital is not vanishing — it is rerouting toward Qiddiya, Diriyah, AlUla, and the Riyadh entertainment ring, which now have an even louder share of the giga-project budget. The Gulf is pivoting from concrete to content.
rebrief the gcc pipeline around qiddiya, diriyah, alula this week.
Dubai is putting 295,000 companies on agentic AI.
Sheikh Hamdan approved a plan on Wednesday to roll agentic AI through 295,000 private firms in Dubai under a 90-day mobilisation sprint, with the DIFC AI-native financial centre as the showpiece. This is the largest single B2B agentic deployment announced anywhere — and every one of those companies will need a face, a voice, a brand layer on top of the agent. That layer is exactly what we sell.
position arlab as the creative skin for dubai's agentic stack.
Microsoft built its way off OpenAI in one drop.
On Wednesday, Microsoft AI released seven new MAI models — image, voice, and reasoning — and routed them into Copilot and the 11,000-model Foundry catalog alongside Claude Opus 4.8. The framing was deliberately about cost and dependency: cheaper-to-serve, in-house, and not OpenAI. Every hyperscaler now has a sovereign model story, which is the same logic HUMAIN is running locally — buy the stack, don't rent it.
add a microsoft mai fallback to every live creative pipeline.
Higgsfield turned video generation into an agent call.
Higgsfield shipped a public MCP server on Thursday that lets Claude, Cursor, or any agent generate cinematic video on Sora, Veo, Kling, and Seedance from a single tool call. Brief-to-asset just collapsed from a workflow into a function. The studios that script this layer first will deliver a campaign while everyone else is still opening a tab.
wire higgsfield mcp into the studio's agent layer before friday.

VIIPatterns
where the whole field is heading
Global
Gaussian splats are leaving the lab and becoming the default 3D format.
Inside thirty days: Niantic open-sourced SPZ 4, Apple deployed splats inside Apple Maps Flyover, Three.js shipped baseline WebGPU splat rendering, and faster-gaussian-splatting hit GitHub. Photogrammetry is over. Every "3D scene" we deliver from this quarter onward should be a splat by default, not a mesh.
Global
Every serious player is now building its own model stack — neutrality is dead.
Microsoft just shipped seven MAI models, Apple doubled down on Foundation Models, Anthropic and OpenAI filed S-1s ten days apart, Tencent is stalling, and HUMAIN is buying sovereign. The era of "we just call an API" is closing; clients now ask whose model is running their experience and where the inference sits.
Global
AR hardware is becoming an ecosystem game, not a device race.
Apple opened visionOS to third-party tracked accessories, Acer joined the field at $300–$500, Meta is prepping four SKUs, Snap Specs launches this fall. The platform play is no longer one killer device but the bundle of glasses + props + branded inputs around it — and creative tech studios get to design the bundle.
GCC · our region
The Gulf is rotating capital from glass towers to intelligent layers.
NEOM paused The Line and is paying out a $16B exit bill. Dubai approved an agentic-AI plan for 295,000 firms. Qiddiya, Diriyah, AlUla are absorbing the budget that was supposed to go into Saudi megastructure. The story of 2026 in the GCC is not how big you can build — it is how intelligent you can make what's already standing.
