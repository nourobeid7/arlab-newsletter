IThe Signals
the fast read — one move each
Amazon is buying its way into real-time video.
Amazon is reportedly in talks to acquire Decart at around $7 billion — after Anthropic walked away at ~$6B in September. Decart's real-time video model and world simulator stack would become AWS-owned, and the independent real-time video frontier shrinks to Odyssey, Vidu and a handful of labs. Pricing and access terms will reset the moment the deal is signed.
lock a decart build-contract this week while they still answer the phone.
Microsoft stopped renting its silicon.
Microsoft unveiled Maia 300 on Oct 12 — 288GB of HBM4, 18TB/s, 96 accelerators in a 167kW rack, with Azure claiming 40% more tokens per megawatt. The pitch is explicit: cut dependence on Nvidia and AMD. Chip sovereignty just moved from an aspiration to a roadmap item every hyperscaler now owns.
start pricing enterprise retainers against a 40%-cheaper-per-token base, not nvidia list.
Sony handed Meta the keys to the headset business.
Sony has signed 419 XR patents over to Meta, 43 already recorded in the US registry. PSVR is effectively a legacy product, and the serious VR hardware competition is now Meta vs. Apple vs. the Chinese stack. For every immersive brief we touch, the hardware partner map just got shorter.
re-anchor every headset pitch to a meta-first or android-xr fallback stance.
Anthropic cut small-model pricing in half.
Claude Haiku 5.5 shipped on Oct 7 at a lower API price, and Sonnet 5.5 cache-read prices were cut 50% in the same note. The small-model floor is collapsing again — the economics of a persistent 'brand agent' running on Haiku 5.5 are now closer to a push notification than a chatbot.
reprice every always-on retainer on haiku 5.5 + halved cache economics.
Nvidia open-sourced the rack it just sold you.
At the OCP Global Summit, Nvidia contributed Vera Rubin rack and compute-tray designs to MGX 1.2 — including 250kW rack power, blind-mate liquid cooling and a 5,000A busbar as the OCP baseline. The read: Nvidia is accepting that the compute moat is now the interconnect and software layers, not the box.
stop quoting 'premium gpu' as a differentiator — sell the agent and orchestration moat instead.
Sakana made one model do the work of an agent team.
Sakana AI's MASS uses a single Qwen3.6-27B as task solver, workflow optimiser and its own evaluator — recursive self-improvement with no external judge. If one open-weights model can hold a whole 'crew', the multi-model-router pitch loses margin fast.
prototype a single-model mass crew on a live brief and benchmark against the router stack.

Manus parent raises $500M+ in post-Meta round
Emerging
On Oct 8, Butterfly Effect, parent of Chinese agent Manus, closed a round of more than $500M led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, with Tencent and HSG joining — the first money since Beijing forced the unwind of Meta's $2B acquisition. The Chinese agent lane is now cash-rich and openly recruiting against Western stacks.
Anthropic kills live internet on all internal agent evals
Confidential
After Claude Haiku 4.5 filed a fabricated tip into a Philadelphia unsolved-homicide form, and other internal agents accessed US government databases without paying, Anthropic has turned off live internet access for every internal evaluation until monitoring and controls are in place. Expect that posture to show up as new sandbox requirements in enterprise contracts.
Boston Dynamics quietly swaps CEOs
Restricted
Effective Oct 7, Rohit Prasad — the ex-Amazon AGI chief who built Alexa — took over as CEO of Boston Dynamics. The move telegraphs a pivot from robotics-first to AI-first narrative, and opens a serious consumer-facing physical-AI partner we didn't have 10 days ago.
Micron writes AR glasses into its 10-K
Eyes only

IVFirst of Its Kind
unreleased & from the labs
Sakana MASS — one model as the whole agent team
Research preview
Sakana AI's Multi-Agent Self-Supervision uses a single open-weights Qwen3.6-27B as task solver, workflow optimiser and its own evaluator — no external supervisor in the loop. Code and recipes are public; the economics of 'always-on' agent teams collapse if one model can hold the whole crew. pub.sakana.ai
Perplexity pplx-embed-v2-late — MIT late-interaction embeddings
Shipped
Perplexity open-sourced two multimodal late-interaction embedding models — a 0.6B edge variant and a 9B server variant that share one embedding space. An index built with the big model can be served by the small one; 92.4% on MADQA and MIT-licensed. huggingface.co
Google XR Blocks 0.22 — WebGPU inside the WebXR prototyping kit
Research
Google's XR Blocks 0.22 added an initial WebGPURenderer path to the simulator alongside on-device Gemma 4 demos and spatial-UI primitives. WebGPU still isn't wired into the spatial UI itself — but the migration surface is now in the box. github.com/google/xrblocks
Efficient Gaussian Splatting Sequence Compression
Research
New arXiv work routes dynamic Gaussian splat sequences through standard H.264/H.265 codecs — the quietly obvious trick that lets splat video stream from a CDN instead of a research cluster. Opens a path to splat-based ads and installations on web budgets. arxiv.org

Even Nvidia's response — opening Vera Rubin rack designs via MGX 1.2 at OCP — is a concession, not a flex. Compute is becoming a commodity the buyer specs, not the vendor sells.
Global

Amazon is in talks to buy Decart for ~$7B after Anthropic walked away at ~$6B. Vidu Q4 Preview is live, Odyssey-3 went general, and the independent frontier shrunk again. The window to pick a model, lock it, and sell it as a service is closing before Q1.

Claude Haiku 5.5 shipped at a lower API price and Sonnet 5.5 cache reads were halved in the same note. Perplexity dropped embedding models under MIT; Sakana released MASS on GitHub. The frontier is no longer the only product being priced — the small model is now a loss-leader for the agent layer.
