KPMG's agentic-AI report had to be quietly pulled.
Restricted
KPMG withdrew its flagship report on agentic AI after researchers found multiple fabricated case studies and citations apparently produced by the very AI it was celebrating. The Big Four are not immune from hallucinations, which is a useful fact when negotiating a pitch against a consultancy.
Twenty-million-track training set is now in court discovery.
Eyes only
Discovery in the Suno and Udio copyright cases revealed at least four datasets totalling over 20M tracks have been quietly shared among AI music developers. Every brand using generative audio in a campaign now sits one subpoena away from a licensing problem.
PhysicsX quietly turned simulation into an AI category.
Emerging
PhysicsX raised $300M Series C at a $2.4B valuation, backed by NVIDIA and Siemens, to industrialise physics-AI for aerospace and automotive. World models are not just for games — they're becoming engineering infrastructure with real budgets attached.
ChatGPT learned to do work while you sleep.
Confidential
OpenAI shipped Scheduled Tasks in ChatGPT on June 17 with almost no fanfare — agents that wake up, run a job, and report back on a cron. This is the quiet move from chat surface to always-on system, and it changes how brand content monitoring should be built.

The frontier labs have decided the model itself is a commodity — the margin lives in owning a vertical and the customer relationship.
