At Apsara on Sept 22, Alibaba unveiled a new custom AI chip, rebuilt its cloud stack, dropped the Qwen4 family (Max, Plus, Flash, 27B) and laid out a public roadmap to a 5–10 trillion parameter Qwen 5. Meta will ship a smart-glasses variant with no camera at all.
Ahead of Connect, multiple outlets confirmed Meta is preparing a camera-less Ray-Ban SKU after 24 months of privacy backlash and shipments-drag reports. The category leader is now hedging the sensor itself — capture is optional, always-on AI is not.

Google gave every AI agent root access to the smart home.
On Sept 16, Google opened Home MCP early access, letting third-party AI agents read state and control lights, thermostats and cameras through the Model Context Protocol. MCP just moved from developer tools into the physical space — the house is the app.
prototype an mcp-driven ambient install for a gcc hospitality or retail brief.

On Sept 22, Tencent released Hy Image 3.5 Preview with 20 reference images, native 4K output and multi-round editing, priced from $0.024 a 2K image on Tencent Cloud. HK shares jumped over 7% intraday — the Chinese image race is now a same-day arms race, not a quarterly one.

On Sept 17, LIONS announced LIONS Riyadh in partnership with Saudi's Ministry of Media, a new regional festival landing in 2027. The Kingdom's soft-power play just captured the global creative-awards podium — every GCC agency will restructure its award calendar around it.

Snap put Nvidia, AWS and Salesforce agents on Specs, priced cellular data by the month, and let Meta take the consumer-fashion lane. The pitch to buyers is no longer 'wear this' — it's 'run your CRM on this face-out.' Every AR studio has to pick a side: agent runtime or brand novelty.

Alibaba and Tencent shipped on the same day (Sept 22): custom silicon + Qwen4 + a public 10T roadmap, countered by 4K Hy Image 3.5 within hours. This isn't a model-vs-model race anymore — it's a factory-vs-factory one, and the West's answer is more compute, not more speed.

In three weeks, MCP has picked up Google Home, Snap Ads, Salesforce and a growing list of first-party servers. The interesting question is no longer 'which model' — it's 'which MCP surface do we own,' because whoever owns the surface owns the session.
