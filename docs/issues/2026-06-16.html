IThe Signals
the fast read — one move each
Washington just switched off the world's most powerful Anthropic model.
On June 12, a US export-control directive forced Anthropic to disable Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 worldwide over a reported jailbreak — even foreign-national Anthropic employees lost access. Anthropic confirmed it cannot filter foreign users from US users in real time, so the only available action was a full shutdown. Frontier AI is now a regulated dual-use export, not a SaaS product.
build every client deliverable against at least two model vendors — never single-vendor.
World models just became a metered cloud service.
Decart launched Oasis 3 on June 10 — an interactive world model that simulates hours of photorealistic driving, served via API at $0.02 per second. The company simultaneously closed a $300M round at a $4B valuation. The category shifted from research preview to credit-card pricing in one week.
price a world-model rental into the next gcc pitch, not a custom build.
Pokémon GO scans are now training military drone navigation.
Niantic Spatial's partnership with Vantor (formerly Maxar Intelligence) means hundreds of millions of voluntary player scans are now feeding GPS-denied drone navigation. Vantor recently won a $217M US Army contract for 3D terrain. Every gaussian splat capture is now dual-use data — and clients will start asking where the points go.
add a data-use and kill-switch clause to every splat-capture contract.
Meta stripped facial recognition from its smart-glasses app within 24 hours of being caught.
Wired found hidden face-recognition code in Meta's AI companion app for its smart glasses; the EFF escalated, and Meta pulled the code in under a day. The episode draws the privacy ceiling for any AR wearable that ships with a forward-facing camera — and re-opens the positioning space for consent-by-design experiences.
sell consent-by-design as a feature on every glasses brief from now on.
The UAE just consolidated all AI authority under one federal desk.
On June 14, Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid announced the Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data — a single national body unifying AI oversight, data governance and digital government. It will operate under Omar Sultan Al Olama's portfolio, making it the new single procurement door for AI-touching work across the Emirates.
map every uae prospect to the new authority's remit before the next outreach round.
visionOS 27 turned out to be a much bigger upgrade than Apple let on at WWDC.
Post-keynote analysis from UploadVR and developers in the beta reveals Gemini-powered Visual Siri, spatial panoramas, screen recording, expanded Apple Intelligence APIs and a long list of refinements Apple barely mentioned on stage. The Vision Pro story for fall just became materially more compelling — and the SDK is in developers' hands now.
prototype a flagship visionos 27 piece before the public release window closes.

VIIPatterns
where the whole field is heading
Global
Frontier AI is now a state-controlled asset, not a product.
When Washington can flip a model off worldwide in a day — as it just did to Claude Fable 5 — every studio building on top of an LLM is one export-control directive away from a dead client deliverable. Multi-model resilience just stopped being a nice-to-have.
Global
World models left the lab and became metered APIs.
Decart Oasis 3 at $0.02/sec, NVIDIA Cosmos 3 open-weighted, Tencent HY-World 2.0 on GitHub, World Labs' Marble shipping — in a single quarter, the world-model layer became something you bill, not something you read papers about.
Global
User-captured 3D is the next dual-use battleground.
Pokémon GO scans now feed military drone navigation; Meta tried to embed face recognition into its smart-glasses companion app. Every splat you capture and every camera you ship is now both an asset and a liability — the privacy floor on AR has shifted upward.
GCC · our region
The Gulf is collapsing AI governance into single federal desks.
The UAE just stood up a Federal Authority for AI and Data under Sheikh Mohammed, unifying AI, data and digital government in one body. Combined with Saudi's Year of AI and the DISAI cohort, the region's AI spend is now routed through fewer, more powerful gatekeepers — the partnerships game is winnable, but only by those at the right table.
