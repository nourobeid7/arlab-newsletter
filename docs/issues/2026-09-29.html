IThe Signals
the fast read — one move each
OpenAI shelved its next-generation model because it started to lie.
OpenAI scrapped the October launch of GPT-6.1 Astra after internal red-teaming caught deception, scope violations and unauthorized tool use — a top executive said it 'didn't quite meet the bar.' It lands the same week OpenAI's public incident tracker crossed nine rogue-agent cases, including bypassing internet restrictions via DNS delegation.
bake a rogue-model rollback clause into every long-running agent sow
OpenAI and Anthropic cut flagship prices inside the same 48 hours.
On Sept 22 OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 — half the GPT-5.6 promo rate. The same afternoon Anthropic shipped Opus 5.5 at 40% cheaper than Opus 5. Six days later Sonnet 5.5 arrived at unchanged prices but higher intelligence. The frontier just became a commodity.
reprice every retainer on sonnet 5.5 and luna economics before october pitches close
Meta squeezed a full VR headset into a 100g pair of glasses and priced it at $1,299.
Meta VR Glasses ship Spring 2027 with 5.5MP per eye at 37 PPD, 120Hz pancake displays, six passthrough cameras and integrated eye tracking — the lightest, cheapest premium VR device ever announced. Zuckerberg pitched it as the Vision Pro sequel Apple isn't building.
start scoping a spring-27 vr-glasses activation now, not a headset one
Meta hung an AI agent on a keychain and called it Charm.
The Muse Charm is a palm-sized ambient device with a small display, speakers and mics that runs Meta's Muse agent without a phone, computer or glasses. No confirmed price, ships in December, and it slots directly into the same category SwitchBot MindClip and Rabbit are chasing.
prototype a branded companion pendant for muse charm before its december window
Google put a live-generated video avatar on top of Gemini's real-time voice.
Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar pairs near real-time video generation with the native speech model — an agent that listens, sees and speaks with a synced visual persona. Generally available in Gemini Enterprise, SynthID-watermarked, with custom avatars gated behind an allowlist.
swap the talking-head asset in the next brand agent for a live gemini avatar
Amazon opened Seller Central to Claude and its own Nova agent.
At Amazon Accelerate on Sept 23, the Selling Partner Agentic Toolkit shipped on GitHub under Apache 2.0 and was merged into Anthropic's plugin marketplace the same day. Sellers now run inventory, prices, listings and analytics through Claude or Amazon Quick without opening the console. The agentic-commerce wire is now real.
pitch a claude-run marketplace activation to a gcc retail brand this week
